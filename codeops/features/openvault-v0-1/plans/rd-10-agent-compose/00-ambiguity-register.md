## Resolution Notes

**AR-1/2:** `apps/agent` is a new workspace package; RD-10 delivers `ov-agent` and the scaffold backend route, while RD-11 owns the `ov` command and credential storage.

**AR-3/4/5:** Sidecar is the default; wrapper is opt-in; `--once` is for CI. Environment names match the published Compose example. Strict YAML, a 30s–24h refresh, and 1–100 entries.

**AR-6/7/8:** Safe filenames, four modes, default `0400`, raw-byte manifest hash, one atomic `identity.json`, a managed-file ledger, and descriptor-based secure reads.

**AR-9/10/31:** One join key/CSR with transient retry; two-thirds renewal with jitter, chunked long timers, five-minute pending-key reuse, explicit `renewal_not_due`/`renewal_not_current` handling; single-flight refresh and cancellation.
